Prayer Sealed

Because Prayer Can't Wait™ Launching December 2026

Private, end-to-end encrypted prayer for Android and iOS, built on post-quantum cryptography (X-Wing hybrid KEM, ML-DSA-65, AES-256-GCM). Private prayers, prayer partners, prayer groups, and recovery designed so your prayers stay yours — sealed on your device, unreadable to the server.

Prayer should never become a product

Prayer Sealed Ministries seeks to expand access to the Christian practice of prayer by providing believers with a private, freely available digital environment in which they can pray for others, remember answered prayer, reflect on God's faithfulness, and participate in prayer with Christian community.

Digital technology increasingly mediates daily life, yet many digital platforms are built around advertising, engagement metrics, public sharing, and collection of personal information. Prayer Sealed is designed around a different premise: technology should support prayer without distracting from it, commercializing it, or requiring Christians to turn private spiritual life into social-media content.

Prayer Sealed is being built to remain free to use, without advertising and without selling users' personal information. The technology is the means; prayer, Christian fellowship, and remembering God's faithfulness are the ministry.

What it does

Sealed on your device

Prayers are encrypted on your phone before they reach us — X-Wing hybrid KEM, ML-DSA-65, AES-256-GCM. We hold the ciphertext and cannot read it.

Private prayers

Keep a request to yourself, or to one person. What you write stays yours until you decide otherwise.

Prayer partners and groups

Add someone by their handle, or carry a request to a whole circle at once. Everyone you share with can read what you wrote, so share to the size of room you mean.

Recovery that keeps the seal

A 12-word passphrase, generated on your device and never sent to us, restores your account on a new phone. Keep it safe — we cannot reset it for you.
