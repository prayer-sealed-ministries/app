4. Privacy and Encryption

Please review our Privacy Policy to understand what information the service handles.

Important. Prayer Sealed uses post-quantum end-to-end encryption. Your prayer content is encrypted on your device and can only be read by you and the people you choose to share with — we cannot read it. Anyone you share a prayer with can see its content, and metadata such as timing and participants is visible to our servers. Use discretion when sharing highly sensitive personal details.

What encryption does not protect against. Someone you shared a prayer with can screenshot it, copy it, or tell someone else. Encryption protects your content in transit and at rest; it cannot control what a person you trusted does with it.
