Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Your Privacy Matters

Prayer Sealed is operated by Prayer Sealed Ministries, Inc., a Florida not-for-profit corporation (“we,” “us,” “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, what we can and cannot see, and what happens to your data.

Important notice. Prayer Sealed uses post-quantum end-to-end encryption. Your prayer content is encrypted on your device and can only be read by you and the people you choose to share it with — we cannot read it. However, anyone you share a prayer with can see its content, and information such as when you post and who you connect with (metadata) is not hidden from our servers. Use discretion when sharing highly sensitive personal details, especially in prayers shared with larger groups.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect an email address, a phone number, or a password.

Prayer Sealed uses anonymous authentication. There is no sign-up form asking who you are.

Account information

  • A handle (username) you choose. Handles are public — they are how other people find and add you as a prayer partner.
  • A display name you choose, shown to people you share with.
  • A recovery identifier and a server-held encrypted recovery record, unlocked only by a 12-word passphrase that is generated on your device and never sent to us.

If you previously entered an email address, phone number, or location into your profile, that information is stored inside an encrypted envelope we cannot read, and is shared only with partners you chose. The app no longer collects those fields.

Prayer content (encrypted)

Prayer requests, journal entries, encouragements, comments, updates, and praise reports are stored on our servers in end-to-end encrypted form. We cannot read this content — it is encrypted on your device before it reaches us and can only be decrypted by you and your chosen recipients.

Metadata we can see

Because encrypted content still has to be routed and delivered, our servers necessarily see:

  • Who you share with, and your group membership
  • Invite records and join-request records
  • Timestamps — when you create, edit, share, or pray about something
  • The category and urgency labels you attach to a prayer
  • Whether you have marked a prayer answered, and when
  • Your account record and your device records
  • Notification-delivery data

Device records

Each device you use publishes a verification record — public keys and an enrollment timestamp — so the people you share with can confirm that content genuinely came from you. Device labels are generic (“First device,” “Recovered device”) and never contain text you typed. Revoked devices are recorded on a permanent denylist so a removed device can never be reinstated.

What we do not collect

No third-party analytics. No crash reporting. No advertising identifiers. No third-party trackers or SDKs beyond the Google Firebase services described in Section 3.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use what we hold only to:

  • Deliver encrypted prayers and responses to the people you chose
  • Connect you with prayer partners and groups you join
  • Send push notifications about prayer activity
  • Verify that content came from the device that claims to have sent it
  • Protect the service from abuse
  • Maintain and repair the service

Operational totals. To run and plan the service, we keep a private operations dashboard that shows totals only — for example, how many accounts exist, how many accounts and prayers are created each day, how many accounts sign in each day or month, and the health of the service. These are counts across everyone. They never include prayer content, names, handles, or who did what, and no third-party analytics service is involved.

We do not profile you, target advertising, or analyze your behavior.

3. Information Sharing

Prayer sharing. Prayer requests are visible only to the prayer partners or groups you explicitly share them with. There is no public feed; nothing you write is visible to people you have not chosen.

We do not sell your personal information. Ever, to anyone, for any purpose.

Service providers. Prayer Sealed runs on Google Firebase — Cloud Firestore for storage, Firebase Authentication for anonymous accounts, Cloud Functions for server logic, Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications, and Firebase App Check (backed by Play Integrity on Android and App Attest on iOS) to verify that requests come from a genuine copy of the app. Google acts as our processor and its infrastructure may store data in data centers outside your country. Google cannot read your encrypted prayer content either.

We may also share information:

  • With your consent
  • To comply with a legal obligation
  • To protect rights, property, or safety

4. Data Security

We protect your information with end-to-end encryption and modern security measures:

  • End-to-end encryption using post-quantum cryptography — content is encrypted on your device, and we never hold the keys to read it
  • Hybrid classical and post-quantum algorithms, designed to resist both current attacks and future attacks by quantum computers
  • Signature-verified device identity, so recipients can confirm who sent what
  • Encrypted transport for all data in motion
  • Device attestation on every server call, so requests from tampered or cloned apps are rejected

No system is perfectly secure, and metadata such as timing and participants is visible to our servers. Use discretion when sharing sensitive details.

5. Your Privacy Controls

  • Prayer privacy. Choose who sees each prayer — keep it private to yourself, or share it with specific partners or a group.
  • Hidden journal entries. Mark a journal entry hidden and it is kept out of your normal list, reachable only behind your device's biometric or passphrase gate.
  • Remove a partner. Removing someone severs contact both ways. They are not notified. You can restore them later.
  • Notifications. Choose what you are notified about, and whether notification text names the person involved.
  • Account deletion. Delete your account and its data at any time, from Settings. See Section 6.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account exists.

Deleting your account permanently removes your private content from our servers — prayers only you could see, your journal, your profile, and your account record. Prayers and praise reports you had already shared with a partner or a group are not removed: they remain readable to the people you shared them with, shown as coming from “[deleted account],” and we keep the public verification record for your devices so that content stays readable to them — because end-to-end encrypted content already delivered to someone else cannot be recalled. Encouragements and responses you left on other people's prayers also remain with that content.

Deletion is immediate. There is no grace period and no way to recover your account afterward.

If you are the only leader of a prayer group, you must close that group before you can delete your account. Closing a group removes it for everyone in it and cannot be undone; leadership cannot be transferred to another member.

Records of revoked devices are retained permanently, because removing them would allow a revoked device to be reinstated.

If you lose your recovery passphrase. Your account cannot be recovered, and we cannot delete it for you. Because authentication is anonymous and any contact details are encrypted, we have no way to determine which account belongs to a person who contacts us. Keep your 12-word passphrase somewhere safe.

7. Content Moderation

Because your content is end-to-end encrypted, we cannot proactively review it.

When you report a prayer, encouragement, or comment inside a group, the report is sent to that group's leader — not to us — and it contains only a reference to the reported item, the reason you selected, and a timestamp. It does not include a copy of the content, because we cannot read it and neither can our servers. Reports are retained within the group.

8. Children's Privacy

Prayer Sealed is for users age 16 and older. Because of child privacy regulations such as COPPA, we are unable to collect the information needed to create accounts for users under 16. If you are under 16, please delete your account, or contact us and we will assist you.

9. International Users

If you use Prayer Sealed from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States and other countries where Google Firebase operates. By using the service, you consent to this transfer.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including access, correction, deletion, objection to processing, portability, and withdrawal of consent.

Most of these you can exercise directly in the app: your content is yours, editable and deletable by you, and account deletion removes what we hold. For anything else, contact us at the address below.

Note the practical limit created by the design: we cannot access, correct, or export your encrypted content, because we cannot read it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy and updating the “Last updated” date. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance.

12. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the State of Florida, United States, without regard to conflict of law provisions.
